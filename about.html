We're building the future of teamwork

Connect started with a simple idea: teams shouldn't need five different apps just to get work done. Today we help over 25,000 teams communicate better, together.

Our Mission

Fewer tools. More connection.

We believe the best work happens when teams aren't fighting their tools. Connect unifies messaging, calls, and files into one focused workspace — so people can spend less time switching tabs and more time doing meaningful work together.

We're a remote-first company building for remote-first teams, and everything we ship starts with one question: does this bring people closer together?

Our Story

How we got here
