FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Our support team is one message away.

General

What is Connect?+

Connect is an all-in-one workspace that combines messaging, video calls, and file sharing so your team can collaborate without switching between apps.

Is there a free plan?+

Yes — the Starter plan is free forever for teams of up to 10 members, with 5GB of storage and core messaging and video features included.

Can I import data from other tools?+

Yes, we offer guided migration tools for Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Discord so you can bring your channels, history, and files with you.

Is Connect available on mobile?+

Yes, native iOS and Android apps are available for free on the App Store and Google Play, fully synced with your desktop workspace.

Billing

Can I switch plans later?+

Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your workspace settings — changes take effect immediately.

What payment methods do you accept?+

We accept all major credit cards, and Enterprise customers can pay via invoice with net-30 terms.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?+

Yes, eligible nonprofits and educational institutions get 30% off Pro plans. Reach out to our sales team to apply.

Do you offer refunds?+

We offer a full refund within 14 days of any paid plan purchase, no questions asked. Just contact support.

Security

Is my data secure?+

Connect is SOC 2 Type II certified. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and Enterprise customers can choose their data residency region.

Does Connect support single sign-on (SSO)?+

Yes, SSO and SCIM provisioning are available on the Pro and Enterprise plans, with support for Okta, Azure AD, and any SAML 2.0 provider.

Who owns the data I upload?+

You do. Connect never sells or trains models on your workspace data, and you can export or delete it at any time.

Support

How do I contact support?+

Reach us anytime through the in-app help widget, or visit our contact page to email or chat with the team.

What are your support hours?+

Starter and Pro plans get email support with a 24-hour response time. Enterprise plans include 24/7 priority support with a dedicated Slack channel.

Still have questions?

Our team is happy to help you find the right fit for your team.
